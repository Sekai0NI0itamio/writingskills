## What This Section Does (content sequence)

A Bibliography performs this ordered sequence:

1. **1st — Broadest secondary web source** (sets the field): introduces an accessible online article to show range.
2. **2nd — Authoritative print textbook** (anchors credibility): grounds the work in a recognised publisher.
3. **3rd — Topical web reference** (broadens subject matter): shows the researcher consulted beyond the first web article.
4. **4th — Primary in-person interview** (introduces empirical data): signals original research has begun.
5. **5th — Follow-up primary interview** (method consistency): demonstrates triangulation with the same interviewee.
6. **6th — Alternative-region textbook** (balances geography/perspective): avoids one-publisher bias.
7. **7th — Digital learning platform** (currency): proves the researcher used up-to-date IB-specific resources.
8. **8th — Theoretical web article** (theory depth): adds psychological/theoretical underpinning.
9. **9th — IB-specific guide book** (method authority): validates the IA-writing approach itself.
10. **10th — Industry/applied website** (real-world closure): grounds the abstract work in a concrete company case.

The WHY of the order: web → print → web → primary → primary → print → digital → theory → methodology → industry. Each move sets up the next by **switching exactly one variable** (source type, geography, or data layer) so the list reads as a layered audit trail rather than a heap.

## Express-Idea Vocabulary

Because the Bibliography has no narrative connectives, "connectives" here are the **structural separators and ordering signals** within and between entries.

- **Sequencing / positional** (no "firstly" etc. — entries rely on): *comma between locator fields* ("Forbes, 18 Oct. 2019, www.forbes.com"), *period to close an entry* ("Accessed 10 July 2020."), *line break to begin next entry* — these enforce ordinal reading.
- **Cause / consequence** — none used; bibliography is non-argumentative.
- **Contrast / concession** — none used.
- **Specification** — *edition marker* ("3rd ed.,"), *page range* ("pp. 1–191."), *date precision* ("18 Oct. 2019") — these specify which version/location.
- **Evidence handling** — *URL quotation* (no connective, but the URL itself acts as the evidence anchor), *Accessed [date]* ("Accessed 9 July 2020") — handles when the evidence was last verified.
- **Explanation verbs** — *no explicit "defined as" or "modelled by"* — bibliography instead uses TYPE labels: *"Introductory Interview."*, *"Questionnaire 1 Telephonic Interview."*, *"Business Management 3rd Edition."* — these label the artifact rather than explain a concept.

## How to Explain an Idea (replication steps)

The Bibliography does NOT explain an idea in the narrative sense. Its "explanation pattern" is **citation-arc construction**: a layered sourcing trail that moves the reader from accessible secondary material → primary data → applied industry evidence. To replicate for any IA topic:

1. **Start with one accessible web article** on the broad theme (no login, no paywall) so the reader can verify the field quickly.
2. **Add one authoritative print textbook** from a recognised academic press (Cambridge, Oxford, IBID, Pearson) to ground the work in credibility.
3. **Insert a second, narrower web article** on a specific sub-topic so the list shows the researcher went beyond surface reading.
4. **Cite your first primary interview** (in-person, dated) to introduce original data.
5. **Cite a follow-up primary interview** (different technique, same or different informant) to demonstrate triangulation.
6. **Cite a textbook from a different country/region** to show the work avoids single-region bias.
7. **Cite a digital learning platform** (Kognity, Quizlet, Khan Academy) to show engagement with current digital study aids.
8. **Cite one theory-focused web article** to anchor conceptual depth.
9. **Cite an IB-specific methodology guide** so the examiner recognises the IA-writing approach is sound.
10. **Close with an industry/company website** to ground everything in real practice; final entry leaves reader on an applied note.

Each step's PURPOSE: to change exactly one variable (medium, region, or data layer) from the previous entry so the list reads as a deliberate audit trail, not a heap.
