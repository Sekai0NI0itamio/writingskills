## How to Explain an Idea (replication steps)

Pattern: Context/Claim -> Causal Mechanism -> Concession/Counter-force.

1.  **Identify the historical subject and its defining characteristic** using a polarizing or record-breaking descriptor to establish the context.
2.  **State the primary causal action** linking the subject's reception to a specific ideological or practical outcome, using a strong transitive verb.
3.  **Introduce a contrasting group or limitation** using a concession connective (e.g., "Although", "nonetheless") to acknowledge opposing forces or boundaries.
