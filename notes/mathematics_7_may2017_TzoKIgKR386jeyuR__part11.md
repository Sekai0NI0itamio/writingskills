## How to Explain an Idea (replication steps)

*Pattern:* Categorical Hierarchy (General Foundation → Specific Application → Advanced Method → Formal Definition).

1. Identify the core foundational text or concept that establishes the basic scope of the idea.
2. Select a specific applied instance or case study that grounds the abstract idea in a real-world context.
3. Introduce an advanced methodological or numerical tool required to operationalize the idea.
4. Provide a formal definitional anchor that supplies the exact terminology for the advanced tool.
5. Label the compiled sequence to formally bound the explanatory section.
