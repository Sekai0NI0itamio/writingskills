## How to Explain an Idea (replication steps)

**Pattern**: *Concessive claim → Chronological evidence → Further contextual implication*

1. **Open with a concession** – Use a contrast word (“however”, “although”) to state that a commonly assumed factor is not the primary cause.  
2. **Introduce a precise date** – Pinpoint the exact day of the event and tie it to a well‑known historical moment that shares that date, showing coincidence.  
3. **Interpret the coincidence** – Use a verb of consequence (“marking”, “signalling”, “indicating”) to explain what the date link implies for the argument.  
4. **Add a supplementary detail** – Begin with an additive connector (“Furthermore”, “In addition”) and provide another factual snippet about the entity’s status at that time, reinforcing the concession.  

Following these steps will produce a paragraph that (a) limits a factor, (b) backs the limit with a concrete timeline, and (c) deepens the context with extra evidence, mirroring the structure of the source passage.
