**Paragraph 1:**
1. "her charcoal smeared glory, and all he could see were the glowing pearly whites of her teeth" — **observation/example** — establishes visual focus on teeth
2. "He knew her face echoed his" — **comparison** — links her appearance to his own reflection

4. "Wearing a human tongue was a vivid reminder of the ​now​, of being alive, of the dark and dirt and death pumping inside you" — **unpack/mechanism** — explains what the hunger signifies
5. "Eyes as wild as her hair, Mary Anne turned heel and marched back into the forest" — **action/consequence** — shows her response to the described state

## What This Section Does (content sequence)

1. **Individual transformation begins** — focuses on one character's physical and psychological change through sensory detail
2. **Group behavior emerges** — shows how the individual change spreads to collective action and ritual
3. **Complete loss of humanity** — depicts the final stage where they become indistinguishable from their environment
4. **External perspective interrupts** — introduces an outside voice that judges and questions the transformation
5. **Internal justification closes** — provides the characters' own explanation for why the change occurred

Each stage sets up the next: personal change enables group action, group action leads to total immersion, immersion invites external critique, and critique demands internal defense.

## How to Explain an Idea (replication steps)

**Pattern: Sensory observation → psychological unpacking → behavioral consequence → external judgment → self-justification**

1. **Start with a concrete sensory detail** that captures attention and grounds the reader in a specific moment
2. **Connect that detail to an internal psychological state** using metaphor or comparison to show what it represents
3. **Show the behavioral consequence** of that psychological state through action or dialogue
4. **Introduce an external voice** that judges or questions the behavior, creating tension
5. **Provide the character's own explanation** that justifies or contextualizes the behavior within a larger framework
