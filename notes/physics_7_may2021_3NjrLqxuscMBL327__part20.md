## What This Section Does (content sequence)

This is an **evaluation/analysis section** of an IB Physics IA, moving through this fixed sequence:

1. **Pattern confirmation** — match the data's shape to a known relationship (exponential). This sets up *why the rest of the analysis matters*.
2. **Strength evidence** — report a correlation coefficient to back the shape claim numerically.
3. **Hypothesis bridge** — link the strength to the prediction made earlier; this legitimises the next analytical move.
4. **Uncertainty procedure** — describe how worst-fit lines were drawn; this pre-empts any reader objection about rigor.
5. **Exclusion justification** — state which data point was dropped and why; this pre-empts the next reader objection about bias.
6. **Range + literature anchor** — give the numerical interval the gradient must lie in, then place literature inside it. This builds the yardstick for self-evaluation.
7. **Self-comparison** — drop the student's own gradient into that yardstick and compute percentage error. This is the climactic numerical moment.
8. **Verdict with caveat** — interpret the error in plain terms, acknowledging a weakness (higher values) while affirming the trend was captured.

**Why this order:** each move establishes the *yardstick or justification* the next move needs. You cannot compare your gradient to literature until you have both the range and the literature value; you cannot defend the range until you have explained the exclusion; you cannot interpret the percentage error until you have computed it.

## Express-Idea Vocabulary

- **Sequencing / addition:** "Furthermore, I attempted to draw the lines of worst fit" — introduces the next analytical step.
- **Cause / consequence:** "This means that despite having higher values" — converts a number into a judgement.
- **Contrast / concession:** "despite having higher values for each of the points" — concedes a weakness before the strength.
- **Specification / scope-set:** "Ideally from these lines, the gradient m must fall" — narrows from procedure to a specific numerical range.
- **Evidence handling:** "The Pearson correlation test that of −0.9602 suggests that" — fronts a statistical test as the warrant.
- **Explanation / framing verbs:** "suggests that our line of best fit shows" — uses a hedging verb to turn raw output into a directional claim.

## How to Explain an Idea (replication steps)

This section uses the **evidence → uncertainty → benchmark → self-evaluation → verdict** pattern. To replicate it for a new IA:

1. **State the pattern your data follow.** Name the relationship (exponential, power law, linear) and say your data mirror it. (Skeleton A.)
2. **Front a statistical test** (correlation coefficient, R², chi-squared). Quote its value and translate it into a strength word plus direction. Link it back to your hypothesis with "as expected from". (Skeleton B.)
3. **Introduce the uncertainty procedure.** Use "Furthermore" to pivot. Describe how you drew the worst-fit / envelope lines, naming the visual cues (error bars, scatter). (Skeleton C, sentence 1.)
4. **Justify the exclusion.** Single out the dropped point by its identifying value, framed as an "anomaly … was ignored". This pre-empts the bias objection. (Skeleton C, sentence 2.)
5. **Quote the numerical envelope.** Use "Ideally from these lines" then write the parameter's range with the inequality notation, and immediately nest the literature value inside it. (Skeleton D, sentence 1.)
6. **Place your own value.** Quote your best-fit parameter value, calculate the signed percentage error, and pair it with "which is a … relative error". (Skeleton D, sentence 2.)
7. **Deliver the verdict.** Use "This means that despite [caveat about raw offset], the [general trend] was captured well." This balances honesty with affirmation. (Skeleton D, sentence 3.)

The logic path is: *I see a shape → the shape is statistically real → my uncertainty procedure is rigorous → literature gives me a yardstick → I sit inside that yardstick → therefore my trend is valid despite a raw-value offset.*
