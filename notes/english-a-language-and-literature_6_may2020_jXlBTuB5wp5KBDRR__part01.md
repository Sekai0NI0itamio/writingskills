## How to Explain an Idea (replication steps)

**Pattern:** Definition → Unpack → Example/Quote → Mechanism/Effect → Implication

1. **Define the concept** clearly using formal language or direct definition.  
2. **Unpack its significance** by explaining how it applies to the context or author’s position.  
3. **Provide an example or quote** from the text that illustrates the concept in action.  
4. **Explain the mechanism** — how the device works to produce an effect (e.g., persuasion, emphasis).  
5. **State the implication** — what this reveals about the author’s intent or the social group portrayed.

Example application:  
1. Define “intersectional feminism.”  
2. Explain how Jauregui embodies multiple identities.  
3. Quote her reference to being Latina, bisexual, and woman.  
4. Explain how this layered identity strengthens her argument.  
5. Conclude that this portrayal validates multi-faceted minority experiences.
