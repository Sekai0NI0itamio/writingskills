## How to Explain an Idea (replication steps)

**Pattern: Definition → Specification → Consequence → Formula → Source → Limitation → Correction → Simplification → Application → Significance**

1. **Define the core concept** — state what the foundational idea is and its primary role.
2. **Specify its key property** — give the exact value, condition, or defining characteristic.
3. **State the consequence** — explain what logically follows from that specification.
4. **Present the calculation method** — introduce the formula or procedure used to apply the concept.
5. **Point to reference sources** — identify where the necessary data or values can be found.
6. **Acknowledge the limitation** — contrast the ideal case with real-world deviations.
7. **Introduce the corrective tool** — present the equation or method that addresses the limitation.
8. **Show a simplified version** — demonstrate how the tool works under common conditions.
9. **Explain the predictive capability** — describe what the simplified tool enables you to determine.
10. **Conclude with broader significance** — state why this entire chain matters for the field or real applications.
