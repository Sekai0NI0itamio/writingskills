## How to Explain an Idea (replication steps)

**Pattern: Authority → Application → Technique → Execution → Result**

1. **Establish the general principle** — Begin with an authoritative source ("In [course], we learnt that...") stating the broad mathematical/scientific concept that applies to your situation
2. **Define all variables and parameters** — Clarify what each symbol in your formula represents so readers understand the components
3. **Connect principle to specific problem** — Show how the general formula applies to your particular case by substituting known values or rearranging for your target variable
4. **Identify computational challenge** — Explicitly state what makes the direct application difficult (e.g., "In order to help evaluating this integral...")
5. **Introduce solving technique** — Present the specific method that will overcome the challenge, defining any new variables introduced
6. **Show the transformation** — Demonstrate how the technique changes the problem from its original form to a solvable form
7. **Execute the solution step-by-step** — Apply standard procedures (integration rules, algebraic manipulation) showing each intermediate result
8. **Evaluate numerically** — Substitute limits and compute the final numerical answer with appropriate precision and units
