## How to Explain an Idea (replication steps)

5. **Substitute the shape/geometry-specific quantity** (here I = ½M(R² + R′²)) on the next line as pure algebra; no words needed.

7. **Cancel common factors across the remaining lines and isolate the target variable** until only the target variable equals an expression in the given parameters.
