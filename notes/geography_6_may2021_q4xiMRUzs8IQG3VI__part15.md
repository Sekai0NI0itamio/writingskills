## How to Explain an Idea (replication steps)

**Pattern used:** *Authority → type → date → access → location.* Each idea (source) is explained by naming who produced it, what format it takes, when it was published and when it was accessed, and where to find it.

**Steps to replicate with a new idea:**

1. **Name the author or organization** — state the surname or institutional name that produced the idea, exactly as printed (e.g., "Ding, Shine").
2. **Quote the title or description** — place the exact title in quotation marks to preserve the idea's framing (e.g., "Forget About China" — How Chinese Companies Can Succeed in Southeast Asia").
3. **State the publication date** — give the day, month, and year (or "n.d." if absent) so the reader can judge timeliness.
4. **Identify the medium** — label the format: Website, PDF, Map, Table, or Diagram, so the reader knows what kind of artifact the idea is carried in.
5. **Give the access date** — state the day, month, and year you last consulted it, establishing the version you relied on.
6. **Provide the URL** — supply the full web address so the reader can locate the identical source.
