# Introduction Skill File

## Purpose
The introduction presents the analytical question or problem the essay will address and offers the thesis as the proposed answer. It also tells readers why the topic matters and what the essay adds to the discussion, motivating continued reading.

## Structure / Moves in Order
1. **Orient the reader** – give enough context so the audience can follow the argument.  
2. **Frame the question/problem** – identify the specific analytical issue the essay will resolve.  
3. **Explain what’s at stake** – articulate why the question matters beyond the obvious and what the essay contributes.  
4. **State the thesis** – present the essay’s claim as the answer to the framed question.  
5. **Optional preview** – if discipline conventions require, provide a concise roadmap of the essay’s main points.  

## Sentence Shapes with Slot Anatomy
1. "Although [commonly held view], this essay argues that [your thesis]."  
   *Slots*: commonly held view | your thesis  
2. "The central question this essay addresses is [specific question], because [reason it matters]."  
   *Slots*: specific question | reason it matters  
3. "In this essay, I contend that [your claim] by demonstrating [evidence/approach]."  
   *Slots*: your claim | evidence/approach  
4. "While [previous scholarship] focuses on [aspect], this study shifts focus to [new aspect]."  
   *Slots*: previous scholarship | aspect | new aspect  
5. "This essay explores [topic] in order to reveal that [insight]."  
   *Slots*: topic | insight  
6. "Because [current gap] in the literature, this essay will [action]."  
   *Slots*: current gap | action  

## Rules (Do's)
- Provide sufficient orienting information for the reader to understand the context of your thesis.  
- Explicitly frame the analytical question or problem the essay will answer.  
- Explain what’s at stake—why the question matters beyond the obvious and what your essay adds.  
- State a clear, contestable thesis that serves as the answer to the framed question.  
- Keep background details concise; defer extensive source summaries to later paragraphs.  
- Match length and preview style to discipline expectations (e.g., Government, Economics may expect a roadmap).  
- If you are new to a discipline, ask about conventions for introductions.  

## Don'ts / Defects to Fix
- **Generic opening** (e.g., "Since the dawn of time…") – fails to signal the essay’s specific focus or stakes.  
- **Dictionary definition** – unless the term is central and unfamiliar, it adds no analytical value.  
- **Broad funnel introduction** – starting with overly broad statements and narrowing down dilutes focus.  
- **Over‑summarizing sources** – detailed source summaries belong in the body, not the introduction.  
- **Excessive detail** – providing too much evidence or analysis before the thesis obscures direction.  
- **Missing stakes** – failing to articulate why the question matters leaves readers unconvinced.  
- **Unclear thesis** – a vague or missing thesis makes the essay’s purpose unclear.  

## Quality Bar
The introduction is compliant if it meets the following checkable criteria:

1. **Orientational clarity** – the reader can identify the general context and specific subject.  
2. **Problem/question framing** – the essay explicitly names the analytical question or problem it will resolve.  
3. **Stakes explanation** – the introduction articulates why the question matters beyond the obvious and what the essay contributes.  
4. **Explicit thesis** – a clear, contestable claim is stated as the essay’s central argument.  
5. **No prohibited openings** – the first sentence is not a generic statement, dictionary definition, or overly broad generalization.  
6. **Proportionate length** – the introduction is concise relative to the essay, with detailed evidence deferred to later sections.  
7. **Discipline fit** – if the field expects a roadmap or preview, such a preview is included; if not, it is omitted.  
8. **Coherence** – the moves are logically ordered (context → problem → stakes → thesis → optional preview).
